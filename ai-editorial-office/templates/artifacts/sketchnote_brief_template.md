# Sketchnote Brief

Purpose: translate an approved `visual_concept.md` into an editorial assignment
for a visual handwritten sketchnote that can be published next to the article.

Output artifact: `sketchnote_brief.md`

Mode: `visual_article_sketchnote`

Source artifact: `visual_concept.md`.

Ownership: created by editorial work and used by Artist Agent or an artist.
This brief translates the approved meaning layer into a handwritten sketchnote
format. The article remains the source of truth, but this brief must not
rebuild the article's meaning from scratch or bypass the approved
`visual_concept.md`. It is not a prompt, not an ordinary illustration brief,
not a corporate infographic plan, not a presentation slide, not a comic script,
and not a drawing-method guide.

Difference from `illustration_brief.md`: `illustration_brief.md` answers "What
image carries the main meaning of the text?" `sketchnote_brief.md` answers
"How would an attentive reader summarize the article's content, structure, and
conclusions on one handwritten sheet?"

Required upstream: approved `visual_concept.md` for
`visual_article_sketchnote`. In this mode, `visual_concept.md` is not an
ordinary metaphorical illustration concept; it is the semantic frame of the
article: central idea, 5-10 key meaning blocks, relationships, author
conclusions, what the viewer should remember, and the emotional or intellectual
feeling of the material.

### Style constraints

- Handwritten article sketchnote.
- One sheet of paper.
- Liner drawings.
- Live, imperfect human drawing.
- Arrows, frames, relationships, and underlines are allowed when they clarify
  meaning.
- No corporate infographic.
- No glossy design.
- No photorealism.
- No AI-collage feeling.
- No poster, ad, presentation slide, comic, or ordinary article illustration.

# Roadmap

This is a deliverable knowledge profile, not a template or pipeline.

## Purpose

Show a meaningful sequence of future progress, dependencies, checkpoints, and
reconsideration points.

## Best Use Cases

- learning, adoption, product, or organizational progress needs order;
- the reader needs direction across time rather than immediate instructions;
- dependencies and milestones shape what should happen next.

## Weak Use Cases

- one-session procedures, stable lookup, or precise implementation acceptance;
- dates or commitments cannot be supported.

## Typical Reader Goal

Know what to do first, what follows, why the order matters, and when to reassess.

## Typical Structure

Starting state, phases or horizons, objectives, dependencies, checkpoints,
risks, and adjustment triggers.

## Strengths

- creates sequence and strategic orientation;
- makes dependencies visible;
- supports staged learning or adoption.

## Weaknesses

- can imply false certainty;
- often becomes stale;
- may hide the work needed inside broad phases.

## Common Failure Modes

- wish list presented as a plan;
- arbitrary dates;
- no dependencies or exit conditions;
- too many parallel priorities.

## Typical Companion Deliverables

- [Longread](longread.md) for conceptual foundation;
- [Implementation Plan](implementation-plan.md) for execution detail;
- [Checklist](checklist.md) for phase-level control.

## Not This

A roadmap is not a [Checklist](checklist.md): it explains direction and sequence,
not a bounded set of immediate completion checks.

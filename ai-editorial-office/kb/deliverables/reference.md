# Reference

This is a deliverable knowledge profile, not a template or pipeline.

## Purpose

Provide reliable, non-linear lookup of stable facts, concepts, rules,
interfaces, terminology, or procedures.

## Best Use Cases

- readers return repeatedly with specific questions;
- information can be organized predictably;
- completeness, consistency, and findability matter.

## Weak Use Cases

- first-time teaching, persuasion, executive decision, or live narrative;
- volatile information lacks a maintenance owner.

## Typical Reader Goal

Find the exact needed fact or rule quickly and understand its boundaries.

## Typical Structure

Information architecture based on reader lookup tasks, definitions, entries or
sections, examples where useful, cross-links, caveats, and freshness notes.

## Strengths

- excellent repeated-use value;
- supports selective reading;
- can become a stable source of truth.

## Weaknesses

- weak learning sequence;
- expensive to keep complete and current;
- can become a taxonomy dump.

## Common Failure Modes

- organized around author categories rather than reader lookup;
- missing cross-links or definitions;
- stale facts with no freshness signal;
- tutorial prose mixed unpredictably into lookup entries.

## Typical Companion Deliverables

- [Tutorial](tutorial.md) for guided learning;
- [Cheat Sheet](cheat-sheet.md) for high-frequency recall;
- [FAQ](faq.md) for observed audience questions.

## Not This

A reference is not a [Tutorial](tutorial.md): it optimizes independent lookup,
not an ordered path from novice state to successful performance.

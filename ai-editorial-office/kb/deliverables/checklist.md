# Checklist

This is a deliverable knowledge profile, not a template or pipeline.

## Purpose

Prevent omissions and make completion of a known procedure or review repeatable.

## Best Use Cases

- the user already understands the task;
- critical steps or conditions are easy to forget;
- pass/fail or done/not-done checks are meaningful.

## Weak Use Cases

- conceptual learning, ambiguous diagnosis, persuasion, or strategy;
- work where judgment cannot be reduced to stable checks.

## Typical Reader Goal

Perform or review known work reliably without missing a required item.

## Typical Structure

Brief scope and preconditions, ordered or grouped checks, stop conditions, and
completion evidence.

## Strengths

- fast to scan and reuse;
- supports consistency;
- exposes completion and omissions.

## Weaknesses

- loses explanation and tradeoffs;
- becomes brittle when context changes;
- can create false confidence.

## Common Failure Modes

- checklist used before the reader understands the work;
- vague items that cannot be verified;
- excessive coverage without priority;
- no exception or stop conditions.

## Typical Companion Deliverables

- [Tutorial](tutorial.md) for initial learning;
- [Reference](reference.md) for rule detail;
- [Report](report.md) when results must be documented.

## Not This

A checklist is not a [Roadmap](roadmap.md) and not a [Cheat Sheet](cheat-sheet.md):
it controls completion rather than future direction or memory recall.

# Speaker Notes

This is a deliverable knowledge profile, not a template or pipeline.

## Purpose

Help a presenter deliver an approved presentation accurately, naturally, and
within the intended time and interaction pattern.

## Best Use Cases

- slide meaning depends on spoken explanation;
- evidence caveats, transitions, timing, or audience questions matter;
- more than one person may present the material.

## Weak Use Cases

- self-explanatory asynchronous decks;
- durable standalone reading or formal documentation.

## Typical Reader Goal

Know what to say, emphasize, qualify, and ask at each stage of the presentation.

## Typical Structure

Slide-linked intent, talking points, transitions, caveats, timing, interaction
cues, and likely questions.

## Strengths

- preserves nuance without crowding slides;
- improves timing and delivery consistency;
- keeps caveats available to the presenter.

## Weaknesses

- useful only with the presentation;
- can become a full script and sound unnatural;
- requires synchronization with slide versions.

## Common Failure Modes

- repeating slide text verbatim;
- unsupported improvisation prompts;
- no timing or transition cues;
- notes drifting from the reviewed deck.

## Typical Companion Deliverables

- [Presentation](presentation.md), normally as the required primary artifact;
- [FAQ](faq.md) for anticipated audience questions.

## Not This

Speaker notes are not an [Article](article.md) or transcript. They are delivery
support tied to a specific presentation sequence.

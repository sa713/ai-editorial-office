# Announcement

This is a deliverable knowledge profile, not a template or pipeline.

## Purpose

Make a bounded event, change, release, publication, or required action known to
a defined audience.

## Best Use Cases

- the audience needs timely awareness and a clear next action;
- a larger artifact or event already exists;
- channel constraints reward brevity and directness.

## Weak Use Cases

- deep explanation, durable policy, training, or formal decision support;
- there is no concrete news, change, or action.

## Typical Reader Goal

Know what happened, why it matters, what to do, and where to learn more.

## Typical Structure

What changed or is available, audience relevance, essential context, action or
link, timing, and caveat when needed.

## Strengths

- fast and channel-fit;
- directs attention to the primary artifact;
- makes timing and action visible.

## Weaknesses

- low explanatory depth;
- quickly becomes stale;
- easy to make promotional or vague.

## Common Failure Modes

- announcement without news;
- missing audience action or link;
- hype replacing useful context;
- trying to compress the full source artifact.

## Typical Companion Deliverables

- [Interview](interview.md), [Article](article.md), [Report](report.md), or
  [Presentation](presentation.md) as the primary content;
- [FAQ](faq.md) for recurring questions.

## Not This

An announcement is not an [Executive Brief](executive-brief.md): it distributes
a bounded update rather than transferring a decision-relevant analytical view.

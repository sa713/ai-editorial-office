# Longread

This is a deliverable knowledge profile, not a template or pipeline.

## Purpose

Build a substantial, durable mental model across several connected concepts,
evidence streams, or changes in practice.

## Best Use Cases

- an experienced reader needs a deep catch-up or conceptual reorientation;
- the topic requires synthesis rather than a single point;
- evidence, examples, and implications must remain connected.

## Weak Use Cases

- urgent decisions, quick lookup, narrow instructions, or short-channel use;
- readers need only an immediate action or approval.

## Typical Reader Goal

Replace an incomplete model with a coherent understanding that supports future
judgment and practice.

## Typical Structure

A cognitive bridge, major conceptual movements, evidence and examples,
practical consequences, and an orientation for further learning.

## Strengths

- supports depth and synthesis;
- can integrate history, concepts, and practice;
- creates durable shared understanding.

## Weaknesses

- high reading and maintenance cost;
- difficult to revisit quickly;
- easily becomes encyclopedic.

## Common Failure Modes

- breadth without a governing idea;
- academic density or jargon overload;
- no bridge from prior knowledge;
- no practical transformation after substantial reading.

## Typical Companion Deliverables

- [Cheat Sheet](cheat-sheet.md) for fast recall;
- [Roadmap](roadmap.md) for future study or adoption;
- [Reference](reference.md) for repeated lookup.

## Not This

A longread is not a [Report](report.md): its primary job is reader learning and
synthesis, not formal recording of findings.

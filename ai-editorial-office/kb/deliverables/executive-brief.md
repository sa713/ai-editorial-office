# Executive Brief

This is a deliverable knowledge profile, not a template or pipeline.

## Purpose

Transfer the most decision-relevant context, findings, implications, and risks
to a time-constrained senior reader.

## Best Use Cases

- a larger report or research package needs an executive entry point;
- leaders need implications and constraints without full method detail;
- alignment is needed before a decision or discussion.

## Weak Use Cases

- the reader needs implementation detail, full evidence, or step-by-step work;
- a specific approval ask should dominate the artifact.

## Typical Reader Goal

Understand the situation, why it matters, what is known, and what deserves
attention next.

## Typical Structure

Bottom line, context, key findings, implications, risks or uncertainty, and next
decision or action.

## Strengths

- efficient and decision-aware;
- preserves an entry point to deeper evidence;
- supports executive alignment.

## Weaknesses

- compression can hide method or caveats;
- may become generic if the decision context is unclear;
- poor as an implementation artifact.

## Common Failure Modes

- summary without implications;
- buried bottom line;
- overstated confidence;
- no link to evidence or next decision.

## Typical Companion Deliverables

- [Research Report](research-report.md) or [Report](report.md) as evidence base;
- [Decision Memo](decision-memo.md) when a formal choice is needed.

## Not This

An executive brief is not a [Decision Memo](decision-memo.md): it may orient a
decision-maker without advocating one bounded decision.

# FAQ

This is a deliverable knowledge profile, not a template or pipeline.

## Purpose

Resolve recurring audience questions and objections through direct,
independently accessible answers.

## Best Use Cases

- real questions recur across onboarding, change, policy, or product use;
- readers arrive with different concerns;
- non-linear access is more useful than a single narrative.

## Weak Use Cases

- the audience first needs a coherent mental model;
- the questions are invented rather than evidenced;
- a formal decision or procedure is required.

## Typical Reader Goal

Find a trustworthy answer to a specific concern without reading everything.

## Typical Structure

Grouped questions, direct answers, caveats or exceptions, and links to deeper
material.

## Strengths

- excellent scanability and selective access;
- addresses objections explicitly;
- easy to extend from observed questions.

## Weaknesses

- weak overall narrative;
- duplicated context across answers;
- can normalize low-value or hypothetical questions.

## Common Failure Modes

- fabricated questions;
- answers that avoid the actual concern;
- inconsistent terminology;
- FAQ used as a dumping ground for missing documentation.

## Typical Companion Deliverables

- [Article](article.md) or [Longread](longread.md) for the coherent explanation;
- [Announcement](announcement.md) for the initial change notice.

## Not This

An FAQ is not a [Reference](reference.md): it is organized around audience
questions rather than a stable domain information architecture.

# Cheat Sheet

This is a deliverable knowledge profile, not a template or pipeline.

## Purpose

Compress already learned knowledge into a fast recall aid used during real work.

## Best Use Cases

- readers understand the domain but forget commands, distinctions, or cues;
- speed and scanability matter during execution;
- a larger learning artifact already exists or is unnecessary.

## Weak Use Cases

- first-time learning, nuanced decisions, or formal evidence transfer;
- content whose compression would hide critical caveats.

## Typical Reader Goal

Recall the right concept, command, pattern, or warning at the moment of use.

## Typical Structure

Grouped high-value cues, compact examples, warnings, and pointers to deeper
material.

## Strengths

- very fast to use;
- improves retention and repeated application;
- complements deep material without repeating it fully.

## Weaknesses

- context is intentionally thin;
- can become cryptic or unsafe;
- requires maintenance when underlying guidance changes.

## Common Failure Modes

- trying to teach the whole subject in compressed form;
- dense wall of abbreviations;
- missing caveats or provenance;
- duplication instead of purposeful recall design.

## Typical Companion Deliverables

- [Longread](longread.md) or [Tutorial](tutorial.md) as the learning source;
- [Reference](reference.md) for full detail.

## Not This

A cheat sheet is not a [Checklist](checklist.md): it supports memory, while a
checklist controls whether required work was completed.

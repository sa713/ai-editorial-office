# Article

This is a deliverable knowledge profile, not a template or pipeline.

## Purpose

Explain, interpret, or argue a focused idea for a defined audience in a
coherent editorial narrative.

## Best Use Cases

- a topic needs context, reasoning, examples, and a clear takeaway;
- publication value matters more than exhaustive operational detail;
- the reader can follow a mostly linear narrative.

## Weak Use Cases

- repeated lookup, live presentation, step-by-step training, or formal approval;
- dense option comparison where shared criteria matter more than prose.

## Typical Reader Goal

Understand what matters, why it matters, and what conclusion or action follows.

## Typical Structure

A strong opening, contextual development, evidence or examples, implications,
and a clear conclusion or next step.

## Strengths

- carries nuance and narrative;
- balances explanation with editorial point of view;
- works well for publication and sharing.

## Weaknesses

- slower to scan than operational formats;
- may hide decisions or actions inside prose;
- ages poorly when overloaded with volatile detail.

## Common Failure Modes

- too abstract or too long;
- weak scanability;
- generic introduction and delayed main point;
- explanation without a reader consequence.

## Typical Companion Deliverables

- [Cheat Sheet](cheat-sheet.md) for recall;
- [FAQ](faq.md) for recurring questions;
- [Announcement](announcement.md) for distribution.

## Not This

An article is not a [Longread](longread.md) merely because it is long, and it is
not a [Tutorial](tutorial.md) unless reproducible guided practice is central.

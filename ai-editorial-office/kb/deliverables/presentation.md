# Presentation

This is a deliverable knowledge profile, not a template or pipeline.

## Purpose

Support a visual narrative for a live or asynchronous audience with limited
attention and a defined communication outcome.

## Best Use Cases

- a meeting, workshop, pitch, review, or teaching session is the use context;
- sequence, emphasis, and visual hierarchy matter;
- discussion or presenter delivery supplies part of the meaning.

## Weak Use Cases

- durable archival detail, precise requirements, or independent deep reading;
- the content cannot be understood without dense prose on slides.

## Typical Reader Goal

Follow the argument, understand the main evidence, and reach the intended
discussion, decision, or learning point.

## Typical Structure

Audience orientation, narrative progression, key evidence or examples,
conclusion or ask, and discussion/next step.

## Strengths

- strong control of sequence and emphasis;
- supports live explanation and discussion;
- makes the main path visible.

## Weaknesses

- often weak without a speaker;
- poor archival document;
- visual compression can remove caveats.

## Common Failure Modes

- document pasted onto slides;
- one idea spread across too many slides;
- visual polish without an argument;
- missing speaker context or decision ask.

## Typical Companion Deliverables

- [Speaker Notes](speaker-notes.md) for delivery;
- [Executive Brief](executive-brief.md) for archival transfer;
- [Cheat Sheet](cheat-sheet.md) for participant recall.

## Not This

A presentation is not [Speaker Notes](speaker-notes.md): slides serve the
audience; notes serve the presenter.

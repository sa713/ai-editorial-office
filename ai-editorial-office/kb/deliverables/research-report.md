# Research Report

This is a deliverable knowledge profile, not a template or pipeline.

## Purpose

Preserve a source-aware research question, method, evidence, findings,
interpretation, contradictions, and limitations in a reviewable form.

## Best Use Cases

- the user needs factual synthesis across material sources;
- provenance and uncertainty affect the conclusion;
- future readers may need to inspect how findings were produced.

## Weak Use Cases

- a short answer, simple transformation, or decision already supported by an
  existing evidence package;
- the audience needs only a bounded executive action.

## Typical Reader Goal

Understand the evidence and conclusion well enough to trust, challenge, or use
the research.

## Typical Structure

Research question and scope, method/source boundary, findings, contradictions,
analysis, confidence and limitations, implications, and open questions.

## Strengths

- strong evidence traceability;
- exposes uncertainty and contradictions;
- supports reuse and independent review.

## Weaknesses

- high production and reading cost;
- method detail can overwhelm the decision;
- findings may age with sources.

## Common Failure Modes

- source list substituted for synthesis;
- unsupported certainty;
- method and selection bias hidden;
- executive conclusion not connected to findings.

## Typical Companion Deliverables

- [Executive Brief](executive-brief.md) for decision-oriented compression;
- [Comparison Matrix](comparison-matrix.md) when options are central;
- [Reference](reference.md) for durable evidence lookup.

## Not This

A research report is not an [Executive Brief](executive-brief.md): it preserves
the evidence path rather than only the decision-relevant compression.

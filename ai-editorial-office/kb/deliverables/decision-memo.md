# Decision Memo

This is a deliverable knowledge profile, not a template or pipeline.

## Purpose

Enable a named decision, approval, or commitment through a clear recommendation
grounded in evidence, alternatives, tradeoffs, and risk.

## Best Use Cases

- a decision owner and decision boundary are known;
- options and consequences can be compared;
- the reader needs a recommendation and explicit ask.

## Weak Use Cases

- exploratory research, neutral documentation, broad teaching, or unknown
  decision ownership;
- evidence is too weak for a recommendation.

## Typical Reader Goal

Decide, approve, reject, or request a bounded next step with confidence.

## Typical Structure

Decision required, recommendation, evidence, alternatives, tradeoffs, risks,
implementation consequence, and explicit ask.

## Strengths

- highly actionable;
- makes ownership and recommendation visible;
- forces tradeoff clarity.

## Weaknesses

- can oversimplify exploration;
- risks advocacy bias;
- requires a real decision context.

## Common Failure Modes

- recommendation before evidence;
- hidden alternatives or costs;
- vague ask;
- persuasive tone used to cover uncertainty.

## Typical Companion Deliverables

- [Comparison Matrix](comparison-matrix.md) for structured option evidence;
- [Implementation Plan](implementation-plan.md) after approval;
- [Presentation](presentation.md) for a live decision meeting.

## Not This

A decision memo is not an [Executive Brief](executive-brief.md): it exists to
secure a specific decision, not merely to orient a senior reader.

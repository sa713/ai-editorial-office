# Report

This is a deliverable knowledge profile, not a template or pipeline.

## Purpose

Create a durable record of findings, analysis, implications, recommendations,
or status for review and later reference.

## Best Use Cases

- stakeholders need an inspectable analytical record;
- findings must remain connected to scope, method, evidence, and limitations;
- the artifact supports review, governance, or follow-up action.

## Weak Use Cases

- immediate notification, quick recall, live presentation, or guided training;
- no evidence or analytical work exists to report.

## Typical Reader Goal

Understand what was examined, what was found, what it means, and what follows.

## Typical Structure

Scope and question, method or basis, findings, analysis, implications,
limitations, recommendations, and next action when appropriate.

## Strengths

- durable and reviewable;
- supports traceability and governance;
- handles complex findings better than short formats.

## Weaknesses

- can be long and slow to consume;
- easily becomes descriptive rather than analytical;
- may bury decisions.

## Common Failure Modes

- activity log instead of findings;
- conclusions not tied to evidence;
- no limitations or priority;
- recommendations disconnected from the analysis.

## Typical Companion Deliverables

- [Executive Brief](executive-brief.md) for senior readers;
- [Decision Memo](decision-memo.md) for a required choice;
- [Implementation Plan](implementation-plan.md) for execution.

## Not This

A report is not necessarily a [Research Report](research-report.md): a research
report makes evidence method and source limitations central.

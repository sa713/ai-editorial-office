# Outline

## Reader Path

1. Understand that the Framework is methodology, not audit.
2. See the source boundary and KB-grounding rule.
3. Learn the audit architecture and workflow.
4. Review audit areas.
5. Apply criterion catalog.
6. Score with maturity/evidence/priority models.
7. Collect admissible evidence.
8. Produce future Audit Report.
9. Attach KB traceability and gaps.
10. Prepare first audit without performing it in this task.

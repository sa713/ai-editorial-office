- From: review_agent
- To: final_editor
- Status: approved
- Review outcome: approved
- Approved artifact: `draft.md`
- Required finalization: apply the approved answer to `task.md`; preserve the question unchanged; create `final.md` and `editorial-note.md`.
- Do not change: facts, commitments, answer scope, or question text.
- Stop if: finalization would require adding information not present in the reviewed draft.

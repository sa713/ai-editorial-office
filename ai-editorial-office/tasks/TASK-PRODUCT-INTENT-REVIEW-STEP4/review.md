## deliverable-fit challenge

- `report` is sufficient for concise findings, broader analysis, and embedded
  Product Intent blocks.
- `decision-memo` is sufficient when a known owner must choose among intervention
  classes and see tradeoffs/ask.
- `research-report` is sufficient when evidence quality, provenance,
  contradictions, and limits are central.
- A separately named task-local Product Intent Review report remains selectable
  under the `report` reader job when explicitly required; this does not justify
  a catalogue profile.
- No stable fourth reader job or distinct failure-mode boundary appears in the
  tests. Creating a profile would be proliferation, not reader service.

  covers future active `limited/full` results without recursive output sections.
- Selected reader job/output form: explicit and restricted to existing

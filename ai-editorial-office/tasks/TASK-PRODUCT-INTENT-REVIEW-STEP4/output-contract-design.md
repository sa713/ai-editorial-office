# Adaptive Output Contract Design

## Reader-first principle

The output exists to help the task owner decide or validate what happens next.
It does not prove that every internal analytical move was performed. The
selected deliverable/profile determines literal structure; Product Intent
Review supplies a conditional semantic and ordering contract.

## Shared order for active modes

1. Verdict.
2. One main product gap.
3. Next owner decision.
4. Evidence boundary and necessary detail.
5. Production consequence.
6. Editorial/local remarks last.

The first three elements must be understandable without reading the complete
artifact. Method, file lists, full model rows, and disclaimers cannot lead.

## `limited`

Use the shortest form that preserves:

- one verdict for the assigned focus;
- one material gap/risk;
- confirmed evidence versus hypothesis/unknown;
- the next owner decision/check;
- production permission or constraint.

Do not print the full model, all checks, long alternatives, research narrative,
or a product passport.

## `full`

Begin with a compact verdict containing overall assessment, main gap, and
recommended next action. Add only decision-relevant reconstructed logic,
strengths, evidence/hypothesis/unknown boundary, at most one primary
recommendation plus one strong alternative and one reserve, practical minimum
validation when material, explicit owner decision, and production consequence.
Editorial remarks follow all product-decision content.

## Communication constraints

- Use direct management language and plain verbs.
- Preserve a negative/no-build/stop/reroute finding without decorative
  softening.
- State the evidence boundary once, then use calibrated language instead of
  repeating disclaimers.
- Source length does not determine output length.
- Do not expose role names, pipelines, mode implementation, canonical KB owners,
  task state, or internal methodology unless the user asks about the system.
- Product finding remains distinct from operational review outcome.

## Deliverable adaptation

- `report`: compress decision first, then findings/evidence/implications.
- `decision-memo`: decision and recommendation first; bounded alternatives,
  tradeoffs, validation, consequence, and explicit ask follow.
- `research-report`: retain method/evidence traceability, but give the
  decision-relevant finding and evidence boundary before detail.
- embedded review: place the product block before editorial/local findings and
  do not create a standalone companion by default.

## Reviewable minimum

Review must be able to locate mode, selected reader job/deliverable, verdict,
main gap, next decision, evidence boundary, production consequence, product/
operational-verdict separation, and order of editorial remarks. Literal
headings and tables remain optional.

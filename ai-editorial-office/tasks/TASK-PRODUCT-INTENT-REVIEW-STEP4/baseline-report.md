# Baseline Report

## Scope

Checked finalized Step 1–3 Product Intent Review contracts, Deliverable
Catalogue, `report`, `research-report`, and `decision-memo` profiles,
Professional Communication, Chief Editor, Writer, Review, Final Editor,
orchestration/research/review task templates, deliverable tests, and current
worktree state on 2026-07-29.

## Existing foundation

- Product Intent Review already owns adaptive semantic output elements, but the
  current illustrative shape is analysis-oriented rather than a complete
  reader-facing ordering contract.
- Step 3 already separates product finding from operational verdict, requires
  one main gap, production consequence, next owner decision, and review.
- Outcome-first selection already distinguishes requested, recommended, and
  selected deliverables and requires the smallest sufficient artifact set.
- `report`, `research-report`, and `decision-memo` already represent three
  distinct reader jobs: durable findings, evidence traceability, and a bounded
  decision/ask.
- Professional Communication already owns message architecture, bottom-line
  placement, density, caveat preservation, reader path, and actionability.

## Verified Step 4 gaps

1. The Product Intent owner does not yet define verdict-first reader order,
   editorial-notes-last, direct no-build language, uncertainty density, or
   internal-architecture suppression.
2. The three deliverable profiles do not yet state how active Product Intent
   Review adapts inside them.
3. Chief Editor does not yet record the reader job/output form for the finding.
4. Writer does not yet have an explicit prohibition on method-dump/internal
   architecture leakage or source-size-driven output expansion.
5. Review does not yet check decision visibility, priority/order, negative
   directness, uncertainty overload, or output/profile fit.
6. Final Editor does not yet explicitly preserve product-first order and direct
   negative language.
7. Existing conditional templates do not expose the selected reader job/output
   form and user-facing output challenge.
8. No executable test covers the twelve authorized output-format cases.

## Boundary conclusion

The gap is a reader-facing projection and communication contract, not a new
deliverable type or pipeline. Existing profiles are sufficient; the correct
implementation is conditional guidance and tests.

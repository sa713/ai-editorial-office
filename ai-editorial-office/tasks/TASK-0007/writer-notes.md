# Writer Notes

## Inputs Used

- User task brief from chat.
- Local tone rules in `ai-editorial-office/kb/tone_of_voice.md`.
- Forbidden patterns in `ai-editorial-office/kb/forbidden_patterns.md`.
- Operational rules in `editorial_knowledge/10_operational_rules.md`.
- Usefulness dimensions in `editorial_knowledge/31_usefulness_dimensions.md`.
- TASK-0006 launch/onboarding communication pattern for platform distinction and useful-first openings.

## Writing Decisions

- Treated opening lines as editorial tools, not style decorations.
- Made zero-opening a first-class recommendation because many internal posts do not need any greeting.
- Kept examples realistic and close to ordinary internal communication.
- Avoided a giant option list; the draft uses a small matrix by platform, type, and tone/intensity.
- Used `softer internal` instead of `warm` to avoid inviting fake warmth.
- Kept messenger examples shorter and more signal-first than email.

## Assumptions

- The guide is for Russian internal communication.
- The organization uses broad internal channels where fake familiarity can irritate readers.
- The guide will be used by writers and reviewers, not sent directly to all employees.

## Risks to Review

- Some examples may still feel slightly templated because they are pattern examples.
- Neutral formal openings are intentionally less strong but included because formal contexts exist.
- The guide should not overcorrect into cold service notices; review should check naturalness.

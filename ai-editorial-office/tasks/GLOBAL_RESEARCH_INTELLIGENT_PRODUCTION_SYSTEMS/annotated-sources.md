## Source Limitations

- Company sources are valuable but may reflect their scale, incentives, and tool ecosystems.
- AI sources are current but volatile.
- Practitioner product sources are influential but less formal empirically.
- Standards provide stable vocabulary but require operational tailoring.
- Adjacent-domain sources require abstraction before transfer to software or AI work.

## Repeating Causes

- Mistaking visible artifacts for operating capability.
- Optimizing local activity instead of system outcomes.
- Adding process without ownership.
- Adding AI autonomy without evals and guardrails.
- Capturing knowledge without provenance or refresh.

## Critical Note

Most anti-patterns are failed translations of useful ideas. Metrics, platforms, portals, checklists, automation, and agents are all valuable when embedded in a functioning sociotechnical system. They become harmful when adopted as symbols without feedback and ownership.

# Known Limitations — Product Intent Review

Canonical owner: `/kb/product_intent_review.md`.

## capability limitations

1. It does not confirm a market, demand, need, value, or causal effect without
   suitable evidence.
2. Product judgment is bounded by supplied evidence quality, completeness,
   relevance, and freshness.
3. Complex or high-risk decisions may require domain, legal, ethical, safety,
   research, architecture, engineering, finance, operations, or other human
   expertise.
4. Minimum Product Validation reduces one uncertainty; it does not prove total
   effectiveness.
5. One pilot or bounded observation does not prove scalability, persistence,
   transfer, causality, or sustainable operation.
6. Product direction, scope, investment, intervention class, pilot, launch, and
   stop remain product-owner decisions.
7. `limited` and `full` require professional Chief Editor judgment and are not
   automated.
8. The 32-case evaluation suite is representative and adversarial but cannot
   exhaust all domains or execution contexts.
9. Professional Analysis remains an open release candidate; Product Intent
   acceptance does not accept that entire family.
10. Product Intent Review is not a full-cycle product studio, product-management
    function, or universal research/strategy framework.

## evaluation limitations

- The suite evaluates frozen structured outcomes and independent judgment
  records, not stochastic runtime sampling.
- Historical comparison uses saved artifacts because an executable
  pre-capability runtime is unavailable.

## readiness consequence

These limitations are concrete and non-blocking for conditional use. They bound
claims and adoption; they do not invalidate the capability's documented
purpose. A future runtime-sampling evaluation, wider domain expansion, or
broader Professional Analysis decision requires separate authority.

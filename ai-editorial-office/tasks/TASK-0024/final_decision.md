## final decision

TASK-0024 is finalized as a behavioral audit of the editorial system.

The system is ready for the next development stage, but the next stage should
be narrow and evidence-led. The audit does not justify a broad redesign, new
roles, or heavier process. It justifies targeted improvements to entry,
review-scope, and depth control.

- Recommendations remain recommendations only.

## what should happen next

Prioritize implementation planning for these three changes, in order:

1. Preflight gate before production.
2. Outcome-aware review, not only artifact-aware review.
3. Adaptive artifact budget.

Each should be handled as a separate future system-improvement task with its own
scope, safety check, review, and final decision.

## what should not be implemented now

Do not add new roles now.

The audit shows no role shortage. The main problems are entry precision,
review scope, and artifact-depth control.

Do not create a new broad pipeline now.

The existing pipelines are sufficient; the system needs better routing and
depth selection, not more taxonomy.

Do not expand or unfreeze the visual subsystem now.

Visual work should remain controlled because earlier visual/direct tasks show
how easily PDF-to-visual production can bypass semantic ownership. `TASK-0020`
is the positive model to preserve, not a reason to expand visual defaults.

Do not retrofit old tasks.

Older sparse/direct tasks are useful evidence, but rewriting their history would
reduce audit integrity and create noise. Use the audit findings prospectively.

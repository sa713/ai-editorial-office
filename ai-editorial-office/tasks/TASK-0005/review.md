## Summary

The draft meets the onboarding/explainer goal. It explains the system through concrete work situations, puts the task/idea distinction in a visible place, and avoids detailed instruction mechanics.

## Usefulness

Pass.

The reader can understand:

- what Биржа задач is;
- what problem it solves;
- why they might use it personally;
- when to publish a task, respond to a task or submit an idea;
- how basic interaction works;
- where the detailed instruction begins.

## Onboarding Clarity

Pass.

The opening gives a direct definition and then explains the practical problem. The text does not require prior knowledge of the platform.

## Reader Trust And Skepticism

Pass with minor residual risk.

The draft handles the likely skepticism by saying the exchange is not a separate reporting contour and not a replacement for existing agreements. It avoids a values-first opening.

Residual risk: the word `вовлечённость` from the source is intentionally not used. This is acceptable because the task explicitly asks to avoid HR framing.

## Information Priority

Pass.

The most important signals appear early:

- shared place for tasks, responses and ideas;
- visible matching between work and available capacity;
- practical scenarios;
- task vs idea distinction.

Secondary details stay later.

## Structure Quality

Pass.

The section roles are distinct:

- definition;
- usage scenarios;
- task/idea distinction;
- interaction model;
- personal usefulness;
- reminders and boundary to detailed instruction.

No section tries to be both overview and detailed instruction.

## Explanation vs Detail Balance

Pass.

The text gives enough mechanics to orient the reader without fields, statuses, clicks or exception cases. Detail is deliberately held back for the existing instruction.

## Corporate Drift Check

Pass.

No material issue found. The line `Биржа полезна не потому, что “надо быть вовлечённым”` directly blocks the motivational reading. Phrases such as “готовность помогать” and “темы требуют внимания” are operational in context, not slogans.

## Abstract Meaning Check

Pass.

Potentially abstract source goals were rewritten into concrete situations: overloaded teams, available colleagues, ideas lost in chats, unclear ownership.

## Example Check

Pass.

Examples are short and help distinguish task from idea. They do not turn the text into an instruction.

## Critical Signal Emphasis

Pass.

The task/idea distinction is strong enough and appears before interaction details. The boundary with detailed instruction is clear at the end.

# Outline

Task ID: `TASK-0005`

## Working Title

Биржа задач: что это и как ей пользоваться

## Intended Reader Path

1. Reader sees that Биржа задач is not another reporting layer.
2. Reader understands the practical problem: work and free capacity often remain invisible outside immediate teams.
3. Reader maps themselves to one of the scenarios: publish task, take task, submit idea, look for help.
4. Reader learns the key distinction: task has result and execution path; idea needs assessment.
5. Reader understands basic interaction: describe, respond, agree, do, accept or clarify.
6. Reader knows the detailed instruction exists elsewhere.

## Section Plan

### 1. Что такое Биржа задач

Purpose: answer first, in plain language.

Key points:

- shared place for tasks and ideas;
- helps match work that needs hands with people who can help;
- not a replacement for management or a separate reporting contour.

### 2. Зачем она нужна

Purpose: name the concrete problem.

Key points:

- some teams are overloaded while others have capacity or relevant skill;
- useful ideas often stay in conversations;
- Биржа makes these situations visible and easier to route.

### 3. Когда ей пользоваться

Purpose: help the reader self-identify a scenario.

Scenarios:

- I have work that can be done by another colleague;
- I can take a clear task and help;
- I see a process improvement but it is not yet a task;
- I want to understand where colleagues need help.

### 4. Задача и идея — не одно и то же

Purpose: prevent the biggest conceptual confusion.

Distinction:

- task: there is work, expected result, author, potential executor and agreement;
- idea: there is proposal or observation that still needs assessment and possible shaping into work.

Include short examples.

### 5. Как устроено взаимодействие

Purpose: show basic mechanics without microsteps.

Key points:

- Author describes task and expected result;
- employees can respond if ready to help;
- Author and Executor agree on result, timing and format;
- after completion Author checks result;
- ideas are submitted for consideration, not automatically assigned as work.

### 6. Зачем участвовать

Purpose: personal usefulness without forced inspiration.

Key points:

- if overloaded: ask for visible help on work that can be delegated;
- if available: choose useful work and make contribution visible;
- if you see a fix: place idea where it can be considered;
- if you manage workload: see signals of demand and capacity.

### 7. Где подробности

Purpose: boundary and next path.

Key points:

- this text gives orientation;
- detailed instruction explains fields, statuses and exact actions;
- use instruction when creating or taking a specific card.

## Examples To Use Sparingly

- Task: “Нужно подготовить подборку материалов к встрече к пятнице”.
- Idea: “В карточках заявок не хватает поля, которое помогает быстро понять приоритет”.

## Avoid

- value-first opening;
- motivational ending;
- platform field details;
- “повышение эффективности” as unexplained phrase;
- abstract collaboration prose.

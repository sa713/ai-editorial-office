# Writer Notes

Task ID: `TASK-0005`

Owner role: `writer_agent`

## Writing Decisions

- Opened with a plain definition before values to reduce bureaucracy skepticism.
- Translated source goals into operational language: visible work, visible capacity, clearer matching.
- Kept detailed instruction out of the main flow: no fields, statuses, clicks, platform paths or exception handling.
- Centralized the distinction between task and idea to avoid repeating it in every scenario.
- Used examples only as orientation, not as a catalogue of cases.

## Assumptions

- Биржа задач includes at least three basic actions: publish a task, respond to a task, submit an idea.
- Task interaction includes Author, potential Executor, agreement on expected result and acceptance.
- Ideas are considered before becoming work.

Reason: these mechanics are required by the user's task context and are consistent with prior task-local instruction materials, but the final text avoids platform-specific details.

## Known Risks For Review

- The section `Зачем участвовать` must not read like motivation. It should stay tied to personal work situations.
- The text must not imply that the exchange replaces normal management or team planning.
- Examples must remain generic enough not to invent formal eligibility rules.
- The final paragraph must not become a generic conclusion; it should mark the boundary between explainer and detailed instruction.

## Review Focus Requested

Review should check:

- whether the first useful answer appears early enough;
- whether task vs idea distinction is clear;
- whether the text explains personal usefulness without corporate language;
- whether interaction mechanics are clear but not procedural;
- whether any phrase sounds like HR persuasion.

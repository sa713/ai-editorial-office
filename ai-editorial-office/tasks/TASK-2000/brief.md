# Brief

## Task

- Task ID: TASK-2000
- Title: Business requirements for "Dashboard of Employee Hobbies"
- Requested deliverable: `business-requirements.md`
- Language: Russian

## User Goal

Prepare one coherent business requirements document for a new portal section:
"Dashboard of Employee Hobbies".

The document must consolidate three user-provided draft files in this task
folder, remove duplication and contradictions, preserve important source ideas,
and be understandable to business stakeholders, product team, analysts, and
developers.

## Required Structure

The final document must include:

1. Название
2. Краткое резюме
3. Цель раздела
4. Бизнес-контекст
5. Целевая аудитория
6. Основные пользовательские задачи
7. Источники данных
8. Основные сущности раздела
9. Роли и возможности
10. Пользовательские истории
11. Нефункциональные требования
12. Ограничения и допущения
13. Открытые вопросы
14. Критерии готовности документа

## Required Functional Description

The functionality must be described in two parallel, non-conflicting ways:

- role description: roles, capabilities, restrictions, goals, and scenarios;
- user stories: "Как [роль], я хочу [действие/возможность], чтобы [цель/ценность]"
  plus brief acceptance criteria for each story.

## Source Boundary

Use only the three drafts in `TASK-2000` as source material:

- `БТ дашборд хобби.md`
- `БТ календарь.md`
- `БТ хобби.md`

Do not invent functionality not supported by the drafts. Logical but
unsupported ideas must be placed in "Открытые вопросы" or "Допущения".

## Style Requirements

- Clear business style.
- No marketing pathos.
- No technical specification depth.
- Requirements must stay at business logic and user value level.
- Preserve important source limitations.
- Make the document readable without source drafts.

## Acceptance Criteria

- All three drafts are read.
- `business-requirements.md` is created in `TASK-2000`.
- The document is coherent and self-contained.
- Functionality is covered through both roles and user stories.
- Role descriptions and user stories do not contradict each other.
- Source ideas are preserved.
- Duplicates and scattered formulations are removed.
- Ambiguities are listed as open questions.
- One consistent style is used.

## Consolidation Logic

- Treat the dashboard as one section with three modules: travel map, hobbies and
  interests, event calendar.
- Use employee visibility and moderation as cross-cutting rules.
- Keep analytics aggregated and privacy-aware.
- Split product roles into employee, event organizer, moderator,
  administrator, and analytics user.
- Keep role descriptions and user stories aligned:
  - employee stories cover profile, visibility, search, travel, events,
    subscriptions, challenges;
  - organizer stories cover event creation and participant management;
  - moderator stories cover publication decisions;
  - administrator stories cover catalogs/settings;
  - analytics stories cover aggregated insight.

# Claims Used

| Final document area | Source-backed claims used | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Summary, goal, context | Dashboard helps employees find colleagues by interests, exchange experience, join activities, and supports HR/internal communications analytics. | C1, C11 |
| Modules | Hobbies/interests, travel map, and event calendar are treated as connected modules. | C2 |
| Audience and tasks | Employees, organizer/authors, moderators, administrators, HR/managers/internal communications are the relevant business roles. | C1, C5, C9, C10, C11 |
| Data sources | HR/profile data and employee-created content are primary sources; admin/moderator data supports catalogs and moderation. | C4, C5, C10 |
| Entities | Interests, categories, trips, marks, events, registrations, groups, moderation statuses, analytics reports. | C5, C6, C7, C8, C11 |
| Roles | Role capabilities are consolidated from all three drafts and separated conservatively. | C5, C7, C8, C9, C10, C11 |
| User stories | Key scenarios cover visibility, hobbies, search, travel, events, organizer actions, moderation, admin, analytics, and challenges. | C3-C12 |
| NFR | Business-level privacy, role access, moderation, data freshness, navigation, transparency, and integration independence. | C3, C4, C5, C11, C14 |
| Constraints and assumptions | Technical architecture and detailed legal consent are out of scope; unresolved items remain open questions. | C13, C14 |
| Product boundaries | The dashboard must remain focused on interests, events, communities, visibility, moderation, and aggregated analytics; it must not become a social network, messenger, HR evaluation tool, travel-management system, time-tracking system, or employee-control mechanism. | C1, C3, C4, C5, C11, C14 |
| Critical business requirements | Foundation requirements are visibility control, voluntary interest profile, like-minded search, linked interests/events/communities, event participation, moderation, and privacy-safe analytics. | C1-C12 |

# Audience analysis

## overview

The materials confuse audiences. They assume one explanation can convince leadership, align executors, and attract participants. These readers have different questions, different tolerance for abstraction, and different reasons to care.

## leadership

### what leadership likely needed

Leadership likely needed to understand:

- what exactly is being approved;
- why this activity matters for УЭК;
- how much time and organizational attention it needs;
- whether it competes with or strengthens "Выравнивание";
- what outcome will prove it was useful;
- who owns the format;
- what risks exist: low attendance, weak topics, intellectualization, calendar overload.

### what leadership received

Leadership received a concept deck with benefits and atmosphere:

- "без галстуков";
- "единое видение КБ";
- "лидеры мысли";
- "культура";
- broad topic examples.

This may create interest, but it does not create decision readiness.

### likely misunderstanding

Leadership could understand the initiative as:

- a nice internal culture idea;
- an extra meeting;
- a speaker series;
- a lunch-format experiment;
- a thought-leadership project;
- a rebranding of existing "Выравнивание" activities.

The decision object is unstable.

## executors

### what executors likely needed

Executors likely needed:

- confirmed format;
- owner and responsibility split;
- immediate next steps;
- launch date and first session decision;
- copy and announcement needs;
- speaker recruitment process;
- topic approval rules;
- what "part of Выравнивание" changes operationally;
- what they are expected to produce after the meeting.

### what executors received

The email gives several useful ingredients:

- monthly cadence;
- Friday placement;
- 15 minute talk plus 20-25 minute discussion;
- speaker also moderates;
- topic criteria;
- an example teaser.

But these are embedded inside a long synthesis and not separated into "decisions", "open questions", and "actions".

### likely misunderstanding

Executors may not know whether they should:

- start organizing the first event;
- prepare an announcement;
- collect speakers;
- refine the concept and return for approval;
- align with "Выравнивание" owners;
- rewrite the teaser;
- debate the framing further.

The email says "предлагаю", not "решили" or "нужно сделать". The operational status remains unclear.

## potential participants

### what participants likely need

Participants need:

- what it is;
- when and where it happens;
- why it is worth their lunch time;
- what level of preparation is expected;
- whether the session is practical or theoretical;
- whether they may ask naive questions;
- whether they can propose topics;
- what they get after attending.

### what participants would receive from current material

They would receive a broad intellectual pitch:

- cyber security in geopolitical, technological, cultural, and human contexts;
- experts as thought leaders;
- future of the internet;
- digital borders and dark ages.

This may attract a narrow group that already likes conceptual discussion. It may alienate people who need a clearer practical promise.

### likely misunderstanding

Participants could think:

- this is too abstract;
- this is for senior experts only;
- I need to perform intelligence in public;
- this is a lecture, not a discussion;
- this is a culture campaign rather than a useful session.

The participant promise needs to be safer and more concrete.

## author

### what the author likely wanted

The author likely wanted the activity to be taken seriously and not trivialized into casual coffee talk. The density of explanation appears defensive: it protects the initiative's intellectual value.

### what the author did instead

The author over-protected the idea by over-explaining it. This made the communication harder to act on.

## audience-specific communication needed

| Audience | Primary question | Needed artifact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leadership | Should we support this and on what terms? | short manager summary |
| Executors | What exactly do we do next? | executor next-step note |
| Activity owner | How does the format operate repeatedly? | operational concept note |
| Participants | Why attend and what will happen? | launch announcement |
| Future speakers | How do I propose and prepare a topic? | FAQ / speaker onboarding |

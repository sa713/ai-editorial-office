# Compact handoff

TASK-0008 completed.

Core finding: the "кибер-ланчи" communication failed because it was idea-forward, not work-forward. It showed intellectual ambition and possible cultural value, but did not give leadership, executors, or participants a stable operating picture.

What is broken:

- the main idea is buried across deck and email;
- "lunch", "discussion club", "Выравнивание", culture initiative, speaker series, and awareness activity are not hierarchized;
- audiences are mixed;
- practical value is asserted more than demonstrated;
- ownership, cadence, topic intake, speaker preparation, announcement flow, and success criteria are missing;
- the follow-up email adds useful ingredients but does not separate decisions, open questions, and next actions;
- the long example teaser performs intelligence and crowds out decision clarity.

Strongest reconstructed idea:

`Кибер-ланчи` should be framed as a lightweight monthly УЭК discussion format: one 15-minute cyber-security topic introduction plus 20-25 minutes of moderated discussion, used to broaden context, exchange expertise, and produce practical takeaways for employees.

Recommended rebuild:

- short manager summary;
- operational concept note;
- executor next-step note;
- participant announcement;
- FAQ / speaker onboarding.

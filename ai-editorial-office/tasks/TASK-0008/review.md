## usefulness check

Pass.

The package gives a usable answer to "what broke": the communication is idea-forward rather than work-forward, mixes audience layers, delays the answer, and lacks an operational model.

## executive clarity

Pass.

The manager-summary prototype states what the activity is, why it matters, what support is needed, what remains open, and how to pilot it. It avoids the original materials' abstract prestige framing.

## operational realism

Pass with caveat.

The operational concept gives a realistic lightweight model: owner, roles, topic criteria, lifecycle, pilot checks, and open decisions. Caveat: real names, calendar slot, and sponsor decisions remain unavailable in the source and are correctly left open.

## information hierarchy

Pass.

The artifacts separate:

- diagnosis from rewrite;
- leadership needs from executor needs;
- participant invitation from operating rules;
- example communication from governance decision.

## audience alignment

Pass.

The package identifies three real audiences and adds the author as a diagnostic lens. It shows what each audience needed, what each received, and where misunderstanding likely occurred.

## signal-to-noise ratio

Pass.

The diagnosis names which ideas are useful and which signals dilute understanding. It does not merely delete complexity; it assigns complexity to the right layer.

## answer-first behavior

Pass.

The diagnosis and revised artifacts define the activity early. The recommended framing is usable as a compact explanation.

## reader explanation test

Pass.

After reading the recommended frame, a reader can explain:

- what "Кибер-ланчи" are;
- why they exist;
- how a session works;
- who attends;
- what decisions remain.

## special failure checks

### communication performs intelligence instead of transferring understanding

Pass. The package directly identifies this as a central failure and gives examples.

### abstraction replaces operational meaning

Pass. The package names abstract phrases and recommends translation into outcomes or removal from action communications.

### detail replaces decision clarity

Pass. The long splinternet teaser is correctly diagnosed as belonging in a launch artifact or appendix, not in a post-meeting alignment email.

### audiences receive the wrong layer of information

Pass. The audience-analysis artifact makes this explicit and maps each audience to a better artifact.

## remaining risks

- Revised artifacts are prototypes, not approved communications.
- The final activity cannot be launched until owner, cadence, relation to "Выравнивание", and first session details are confirmed.
- If shared with the original author, feedback should be framed around reader tasks and operating model, not personal writing style.

Approved for final governance decision.

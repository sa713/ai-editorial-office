# Rewrite strategy

## strategic decision

Do not produce one "better text". Rebuild the communication architecture.

The activity needs separate artifacts because the audiences need different layers:

- leadership: decision summary;
- executors: operational next steps;
- owner/sponsor: repeatable operating model;
- participants: clear invitation;
- speakers: preparation and participation FAQ.

## primary reframing

Current frame:

```text
Кибер-ланч без галстуков — серия неформальных сессий про широкие темы КБ, культуру, лидерство мысли и развитие awareness.
```

Recommended frame:

```text
Кибер-ланчи — ежемесячный дискуссионный формат УЭК: один короткий вводный доклад по теме КБ и 20-25 минут обсуждения, чтобы сотрудники быстрее связывали текущие кибер-темы с работой банка, обменивались экспертизой и собирали практические выводы.
```

This frame makes the activity:

- a recurring format, not a vague initiative;
- operational, not only cultural;
- useful for employees, not only impressive for experts;
- small enough to pilot.

## what to rewrite

Rewrite:

- activity definition;
- leadership pitch;
- executor follow-up;
- participant announcement;
- speaker/topic criteria;
- example teaser.

## what to restructure

Separate:

- decision rationale from execution mechanics;
- launch communication from internal planning;
- topic examples from operating rules;
- participant promise from leadership benefits;
- open questions from agreed decisions.

## what to shorten

Shorten or demote:

- abstract value claims;
- "prestige" and "thought leadership" rhetoric;
- long future-of-internet teaser in operational email;
- broad lists of possible topics unless used as backlog;
- repeated culture and awareness claims.

## what to remove or avoid

Avoid in primary communications:

- "гибрид-синтез" as a reader-facing frame;
- apologetic "много букв";
- "каждый уважающий себя эксперт должен..." because it creates pressure and status anxiety;
- FOMO as explicit motivation;
- unresolved parenthetical doubts such as "надо ли нам вообще про ланч упоминать?";
- vague claims of prestige without operational proof.

## what to keep

Keep:

- informal, low-barrier format;
- short talk plus discussion;
- topic criteria tied to КБ and usefulness;
- broad but curated topic territory;
- opportunity for employees to speak;
- transparent teaser for each session;
- connection to "Выравнивание" if leadership confirms it.

## artifact architecture

### 1. Short manager summary

Purpose: get leadership alignment.

Must answer:

- what is proposed;
- why now;
- what support is needed;
- pilot scope;
- success criteria;
- open decisions.

### 2. Operational concept note

Purpose: make the activity repeatable.

Must define:

- format;
- cadence;
- audience;
- roles;
- topic criteria;
- lifecycle for each session;
- artifacts and feedback;
- unresolved decisions.

### 3. Executor next-step communication

Purpose: convert the meeting into action.

Must separate:

- decisions;
- proposals requiring confirmation;
- open questions;
- next actions;
- owners and deadlines.

### 4. Participant announcement

Purpose: attract the right attendance without overpromising.

Must answer:

- what happens;
- why attend;
- topic of first session;
- time/place;
- how to join or propose a topic.

### 5. FAQ / onboarding

Purpose: reduce repeated clarification.

Must answer:

- can I attend without being an expert;
- can I propose a topic;
- how long is a talk;
- what makes a good topic;
- what preparation is expected;
- are materials shared after.

## recommended sequence

1. Confirm operating decisions with sponsor:
   - name;
   - owner;
   - cadence;
   - target audience;
   - relation to "Выравнивание";
   - first session date.
2. Create operational concept note.
3. Send executor next-step note.
4. Prepare first participant announcement.
5. Prepare FAQ and speaker guidance.
6. Pilot 2-3 sessions.
7. Review attendance, feedback, topic quality, and repeatability.

## success test

After reading the rebuilt materials, a person should be able to say:

```text
Кибер-ланчи — это ежемесячные короткие дискуссии УЭК по темам кибербезопасности. На каждой встрече один спикер 15 минут вводит тему, потом группа обсуждает, как это связано с работой банка и команды. Формат нужен, чтобы обмениваться экспертизой, расширять контекст и собирать практические выводы. Сейчас нужно подтвердить владельца, расписание и первую тему.
```

If readers cannot produce a version of this explanation, the communication has not yet succeeded.

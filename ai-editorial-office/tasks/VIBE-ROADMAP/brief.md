# Brief

## Task

Спроектировать `VIBE_CODING_ROADMAP.md` — практический маршрут развития участника Vibe Coding Community от первого знакомства с AI до уверенного применения AI в рабочих задачах и передачи опыта другим участникам.

## Source Boundary

Use:

- `../VIBE-VISION/VIBE_CODING_COMMUNITY_VISION_v1_1.md`;
- `https://roadmap.sh/vibe-coding`;
- inspected source/content materials from the public roadmap.sh repository and related roadmap.sh guides.

Do not:

- turn the roadmap into a generic tool catalog;
- copy the roadmap.sh structure verbatim;
- invent new community roles, activities, entities, or strategic directions beyond the approved vision;
- make the roadmap only about software engineering if the community concept is broader than coding tools.

## Deliverable

- `VIBE_CODING_ROADMAP.md`

## Audience

Current and future Vibe Coding Community participants who need a practical path from first experiments to confident workplace use and contribution back to the community.

## Success Criteria

The roadmap should:

- align with the participant path from the vision: novice, practitioner, case author, mentor;
- translate roadmap.sh vibe-coding practices into community-ready learning stages;
- show concrete skills, practices, artifacts, and readiness signals for each stage;
- support real work tasks, not abstract AI enthusiasm;
- help the portal and community formats route people to the right next step.

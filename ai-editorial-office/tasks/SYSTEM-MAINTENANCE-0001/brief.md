# Brief

Task ID: `SYSTEM-MAINTENANCE-0001`

Task title: `Relevance pressure update`

Task type: `governance/system-improvement`

Created at: `2026-05-19 18:44:00 MSK`

## context

`TASK-0003` and `TASK-0003B` executed the local editorial pipeline correctly, but the resulting copy exposed a system weakness.

The system caught:

- corporate sludge;
- fake excitement;
- synthetic warmth;
- HR tone;
- editorial air;
- excessive rewriting.

The system did not sufficiently catch:

- text substituting the base purpose of the magazine/rubric for the specific angle of the current issue;
- lack of release-specific information value;
- high replaceability risk;
- dead mechanical closing phrases.

## update goal

Strengthen review and governance so short-form editorial materials are checked for relevance to the specific material, release, issue, topic, or hero, not only for tone safety and artifact compliance.

## non-goals

- do not change the overall pipeline;
- do not change bounded revision principles;
- do not change role separation;
- do not change compact handoff structure;
- do not change artifact minimalism;
- do not collapse review and governance.

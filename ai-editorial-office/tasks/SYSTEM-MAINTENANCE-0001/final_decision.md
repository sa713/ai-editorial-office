The update is accepted as a minimal governance/system improvement.

## rationale

The previous rules were strong on tone and artifact discipline but did not force review to distinguish between:

- the base purpose of a format/rubric/magazine;
- the actual angle of a specific issue, release, topic, or material.

The update adds this pressure without changing the pipeline or introducing a new framework.

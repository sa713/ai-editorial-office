## writing brief

Create three versions based on the restrained variant:

- `restrained deeper`;
- `quiet human pull`;
- `editorial but not magazine-like`.

Each version should be slightly fuller than the base, but not inflated.

## review criteria

Review each version for:

- usefulness;
- reader-state;
- trust;
- depth;
- human gravity;
- anti-genericity;
- failure patterns;
- synthetic tone;
- risk of fake depth;
- risk of editorial air;
- risk of HR framing.

Each review must also answer:

- which phrase creates a desire to open the issue;
- what cannot be moved into any other internal magazine without loss;
- where the text remains honest;
- where it starts to sound written-up.

## selection rule

Do not choose the most beautiful version. Choose the best balance of:

- restrained honesty;
- human gravity;
- enough pull to read;
- low synthetic tone.

## editorial intent

Do not sell the issue. Create quiet, real interest: behind familiar or half-familiar names there are people with a path, questions, working context, and their own voice.

## editorial challenge

Add editorial gravity, not volume.

In this task, editorial gravity means:

- concrete human feeling;
- a reader-relevant observation that cannot be moved into any corporate announcement without loss;
- soft inner tension: colleagues are near in work, but often only partly visible;
- a reason to open the issue without promotional pressure;
- a sense that behind names there is experience, path, questions, and intonation.

## constraints

- no `мы рады сообщить`;
- no `уникальная возможность`;
- no slogans;
- no `молодые специалисты — будущее управления`;
- no `талантливые ребята`;
- no HR motivation;
- no synthetic warmth;
- no promotional hooks;
- no `повод`;
- no links, names, exact number of heroes unless supplied by user;
- no new facts;
- no invented interview details;
- no long history of the magazine.

## risk mode proposal

Rationale: bounded internal announcement revision using supplied facts only. Main risk is tonal, not factual; review gate remains mandatory.

# Structure From Intent

Task ID: `TASK-0003B`

Owner: `writer_agent`

Stage: `structure-from-intent`

## proposed structure

1. **Issue signal**
   Open directly: issue №13 of `Люди УЭК` is out.

2. **Format shift**
   Place the change immediately after the signal: this time, not one hero, but several young specialists.

3. **Human gravity**
   Use the central tension:

   ```text
   We often meet colleagues through work fragments: a name, a message, a task, a meeting. The issue lets those fragments gain context.
   ```

   This creates a quiet reason to read without selling the issue.

4. **Close**
   End with a calm availability line. Avoid a promotional call-to-action. The close can be plain if the previous paragraph has enough pull.

## structure guardrails

- Do not make the opening more literary.
- Do not add an abstract explanation of the magazine's mission.
- Do not make young specialists a symbolic group.
- Do not imply specific interview content that was not supplied.
- Do not use `повод`.

# Baseline Comparison

## method

An executable pre-Product-Intent runtime is unavailable. The historical column
therefore uses saved task artifacts and the documented absence of the later
Step 1–5 contracts. The current column uses the Step 6 structured outcome and
independent rubric record. No old response is invented.

## key comparisons

| Control case | Documented historical baseline | Current decision-ready behavior | Decision improvement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Systems-thinking learning | Editorial review could improve structure and topic coverage while leaving transfer assumptions implicit. | Full mode separates topic strength from a missing practice/transfer mechanism and selects a short exercise. | Owner decides whether the mechanism justifies the course rather than polishing nine modules. |
| Hobby dashboard/portal | A requested dashboard or mini-app could be treated as the deliverable to design. | Full mode makes separate-product necessity and existing-platform reuse the main gap. | Owner can test observed commitment on an existing surface before funding a product. |
| Biometric banners | Copy quality and compliance could dominate even when refusal cause was unknown. | Full mode separates copy, trust, UX, and product mechanics; local approved-copy contrast remains `not_needed`. | Owner sees when copy is permitted and when it would conceal a product problem. |
| LoveMark | Personalization could be treated as self-evidently valuable, or weak text as the main issue. | Full mode challenges the behavioral mechanism; limited contrast confines review to one approved UX action. | Owner can choose a greeting-only boundary or authorize mechanism validation. |
| AI employee environment | Fluent demonstrations and format lists could be mistaken for work effect. | Full mode distinguishes decision quality, data, variability, human control, confidentiality, and bounded rollout. | Owner can proceed when controls/effect are supported and reroute when a minimum test is insufficient. |
| Large documents | Source length could cause long output or imply full mode. | A large weak campaign receives full review; a large approved BRD with three typos remains compact `not_needed`. | Mode and output length follow the product question, not document size. |

## calibration result

- The current capability discovers risks that ordinary editorial review could
  miss: unsupported mechanisms, solution/problem conflation, wrong
  intervention class, false precision, and authority-as-need.
- Owner decisions are clearer because every active result names one gap,
  evidence boundary, finding, consequence, and next decision.
- Compact negative cases do not grow in length or process.
- Strong supported concepts are allowed to proceed; the suite does not reveal
  systematic over-caution.
- Negative findings remain direct and evidence-bounded; the suite does not
  reveal systematic over-support.
- No result claims product expertise beyond the supplied hidden evidence.

# Product Judgment Evaluation Rubric

## scale

- `0` — absent or contradicts the accepted contract;
- `1` — partial with a material defect;
- `2` — sufficient for the next decision;
- `3` — strong, evidence-bounded execution;
- `not_applicable` — the dimension is genuinely outside the case, especially
  analysis/validation in a correct `not_needed` task.

Scores are diagnostic. They are not summed into a universal quality metric.

## dimensions

| Dimension | Sufficient (`2`) | Strong (`3`) | Material failure (`0–1`) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Activation accuracy | Correct mode and material negative evidence. | The contrast with a nearby mode is explicit and compact path is protected. | Keyword-only, over-activation, missed activation, or mode mismatch. |
| Problem fidelity | Problem is not a symptom/solution and evidence limits remain visible. | Existing workarounds and consequences sharpen the decision. | Invented need/audience or solution stated as problem. |
| Model quality | Relevant elements are sufficient and unknowns remain unknown. | Causal links and disconfirming evidence are explicit without form-filling. | Guessed fields or complete-looking model with no mechanism. |
| Main-gap quality | One decision-changing gap is prioritized. | Gap explains why alternatives, consequence, and validation follow. | Local editorial issue hides the critical risk. |
| Product judgment quality | Finding follows evidence and is proportionate. | Strengths, alternatives, and negative evidence calibrate a direct conclusion. | Unsupported no-build, reflexive support, excessive caution, or owner substitution. |
| Production consequence quality | Permitted and premature work are clear and fit the finding. | Constraints and reroute trigger make the next production boundary operational. | Consequence conflicts with finding or becomes a hidden status. |
| Validation quality | Critical assumption, fitted minimum method, observable signal, decision conditions, and limits. | Validation is clearly the cheapest diagnostic next check or is honestly not needed/insufficient. | Universal pilot, wrong method, full product, invented threshold, or overclaim. |
| Communication quality | Verdict-first, clear, prioritized, bounded, and free of internal architecture. | Direct negative language and adaptive density improve decision use. | Product decision buried, formal method dump, leakage, or disclaimer overload. |
| Authority discipline | Product owner decides; roles stay within current boundaries. | Review challenges without redesigning and finalization preserves meaning. | Product-owner substitution, writer redesign, second gate, or finding/outcome conflation. |
| Overall decision usefulness | Result enables a materially better next decision than ordinary editorial review. | It changes the decision frame while staying proportionate to evidence and task size. | No actionable distinction, false confidence, or service weight without value. |

## automatic failure conditions

Any of these fails a case regardless of scores:

- invented need or audience;
- product-owner substitution;
- hidden critical gap;
- keyword-only activation;
- evidence-free no-build;
- full product instead of minimum validation;
- product finding used as operational verdict;
- compact-path degradation;
- persistent validation-method mismatch;
- second review gate or hidden lifecycle stage.

## manual protocol

1. Read the input and hidden structure.
2. Check expected mode and bounded scope.
3. Inspect the observed result without changing it.
4. Score only applicable dimensions.
5. Name the main defect if any.
6. Distinguish style preference from contract violation.
7. Record confidence and a case-specific rationale.
8. Hand confirmed defects to a separate repair loop.

Independent review must not rewrite a result during evaluation.

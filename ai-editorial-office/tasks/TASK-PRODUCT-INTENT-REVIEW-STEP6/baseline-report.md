```text
input
-> mode/focus
-> evidence-bounded model
-> main gap
-> finding
-> production consequence
-> validation
-> reader-facing decision
-> governance
```

They also do not aggregate cross-case class/pair/adversarial coverage or
preserve a separate manual product-judgment record.

# Decision Integration Design

## Analysis ownership and ordering

For `limited` and `full`, Product Intent Review must produce a sufficient
product finding before Chief Editor authorizes deep editorial production.
Research Agent collects and structures assigned evidence when research is
needed; Chief Editor explicitly assigns an existing analytical owner. Research
Agent may hold that responsibility and form the decision-ready finding when
explicitly assigned, without setting the production consequence or replacing
the product owner.
Writer Agent and UX Writer consume only the approved boundary. Review Agent
challenges the result independently.

Pre-analysis work may recover structure, summarize sources, map evidence, or
create a minimal validation artifact. Deep restructuring, long-form production,
communication series, full-course design, or UX copy for unapproved behavior
must wait.

## Mode behavior

- `not_needed`: no analysis, review dimension, extra artifact, or production
  contract.
- `limited`: one assigned focus, only material model elements/checks, one
  bounded finding and consequence; no full-product redesign.
- `full`: all available seven-element-model evidence, all four checks,
  evidence/assumption/hypothesis/unknown separation, one main gap, bounded
  alternatives, minimum validation when material, product finding, next owner
  decision, and production permission.

## Product finding and consequence

The product finding remains evidence-bounded free text. It is never a task
status or review outcome. Chief Editor maps it to one editorial consequence:

- `Proceed`;
- `Proceed with constraints`;
- `Validate before production`;
- `Reroute`;
- `Stop / no-build recommendation`.

The mapping controls editorial production only. It does not take the product
owner's business, scope, launch, investment, or stop decision.

## Compact Editorial Decision Frame block

When mode is `limited` or `full`, the frame carries only:

- mode and limited focus when applicable;
- product finding;
- one main product gap;
- evidence boundary;
- production consequence and permission;
- owner decision required;
- reconsideration/reroute trigger.

The full model, research narrative, alternatives, and validation design stay in
the smallest suitable existing analytical artifact.

## Minimum validation

When a material hypothesis blocks a decision, analysis records the bounded
hypothesis, audience/context, minimum intervention, observable signal,
continue condition, reconsider condition, and inference limits. It must be
smaller than full implementation and must not fabricate metrics, rigor, or
general proof.

## Production boundary and reroute

Writer Agent and UX Writer preserve the finding, consequence, constraints,
uncertainty, and owner boundary. They cannot repair the product through copy,
add unsupported effects, expand scope, change behavior, or turn a pilot into a
launch. A newly discovered material gap, changed audience/goal/format,
unsupported mechanism, conflicting deliverable, or required behavior change
returns to Chief Editor; production does not redesign the product itself.

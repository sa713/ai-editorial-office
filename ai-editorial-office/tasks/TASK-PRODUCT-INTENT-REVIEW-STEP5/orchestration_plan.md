## editorial decision frame

- Chosen editorial route: deepen the existing canonical minimum-validation section, add short role/profile consequences, and extend the existing executable checker and fixtures.
- Why this route serves the outcome: the weakness is semantic and reviewable inside current owners; no new workflow is required.
- Alternatives considered:
  - New validation pipeline — rejected because validation is conditional advice inside Product Intent Review, not a lifecycle stage.
  - Product Researcher role — rejected because Research Agent can support method/evidence work while the product owner retains decisions.
  - Canon-only prose — rejected because weak signals, method mismatch, invented thresholds, and overlarge pilots need executable rejection.
- Writer contract:
  - Result type: bounded canonical/test diff and reports.
  - Scope boundary: Step 5 only.
  - Must include: gap-linked critical hypothesis, validation disposition, type-method fit, minimum/reversible intervention, observable signal, decision conditions, inference limits, nearest next decision, and special AI boundaries.
  - Must not include: new role/pipeline/stage/gate/status/outcome/report default, routing/mode/release change, or Step 6.
- Review focus: criticality, method fit, minimality, signal strength, qualitative criteria, no invented thresholds, `not_needed`, `insufficient`, sequential overreach, limited focus, and authority boundaries.
- Reroute triggers: any need for a new evidence taxonomy, mandatory research stage, full product research program, or release-state change.

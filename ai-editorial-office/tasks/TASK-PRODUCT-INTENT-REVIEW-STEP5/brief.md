## goal

Strengthen the existing minimum hypothesis validation inside Product Intent
Review so it reliably identifies the critical assumption for the next
decision, selects a context-appropriate method, proposes a genuinely minimum
intervention, defines an observable signal and decision conditions, and states
honest inference limits.

## required behavior

- connect validation to the one main product gap;
- select one critical hypothesis or a tightly related minimum set;
- distinguish problem, demand, mechanism, behavior, usability, feasibility,
  and viability hypotheses;
- match method to hypothesis, risk, next-step cost, reversibility, available
  evidence, audience context, and ethical or organizational constraints;
- specify context, minimum intervention, observable signal, continue and
  reconsider conditions, inference limits, and the next owner decision;
- allow evidence-grounded qualitative conditions without inventing numeric
  thresholds;
- reject weak attitudinal signals as proof of demand, behavior, mechanism, or
  lasting effect;
- recommend only the nearest decision-relevant check, not a full research
  program;
- state when validation is not needed and when one minimum validation is
  insufficient;
- preserve `limited` focus and keep the reader-facing result compact.

## constraints followed

- No publication channel or approval status is implied.
- No internal organization examples are used.
- No vendor-specific, legal, compliance, security, HR, or policy claims are used.
- No numeric productivity, ROI, adoption-scale, or frequency claims are used in the draft.
- Blocked claims from `claims_table.md` are intentionally excluded.

## caveated areas

- The draft says AI can create rework, verification burden, and coordination overhead; it avoids claiming net productivity loss.
- The draft discusses trust as trust in process and predictability, not as a proven universal reputational harm.
- The draft uses research-backed mechanisms in editorial language, with detailed traceability kept in `claims-used.md`.

## intentionally avoided claims

- Most AI work in companies is useless.
- AI makes people stop thinking.
- The user's organization has AI workflow problems.
- Any specific vendor or product causes dysfunction.
- AI adoption creates legal, HR, security, or compliance risk in this task context.
- Productivity losses can be quantified for the target audience.

## fact-use cautions

- Facts from Microsoft, HBR, and McKinsey should be attributed because they are survey/report sources with specific populations and institutional interests.
- The HBR workslop data should be treated as suggestive workplace evidence, not definitive proof that AI reduces productivity everywhere.
- The BCG consulting study supports task-dependent effects, not universal conclusions about all teams.

## claim-use summary

- Strongest usable foundation: task fit, verification burden, workslop/rework, training and workflow-design gaps, trust calibration.
- Safest framing: conditional and mechanism-based.
- Unsafe framing: universal harm, invented internal examples, numeric ROI/productivity claims, vendor blame, legal/compliance claims.

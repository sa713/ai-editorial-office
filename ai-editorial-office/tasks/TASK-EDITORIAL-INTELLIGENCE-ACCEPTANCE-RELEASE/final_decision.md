# Final Decision

## Decision

Chief Editor declares `S5.R5 - Editorial Intelligence Acceptance` ready as a
Release Candidate for Project Lead review.

This is a Release Candidate readiness decision only. It is not Project Lead
acceptance, does not mark S5.R5 `Done`, does not close Stage 5, and does not
start a future stage.

## Governance basis

- The existing Release Pack standard owns the conditional Editorial
  Intelligence Acceptance contract.
- No new owner, capability, role, pipeline, lifecycle stage, review gate,
  board, status, score, dashboard, store, or automation was introduced.
- Value and restraint are independent requirements. Neither can compensate for
  failure of the other, and neither is reduced to a score.
- Synthetic evidence is identified as synthetic and cannot establish
  operational improvement.
- Project Lead retains all acceptance authority. Every contract disposition is
  advisory and performs no automatic repository or governance action.

## Evidence and review basis

- Authoritative research covers AI evaluation, human oversight, assurance-case
  reasoning, engineering acceptance, architecture tradeoffs, continuous
  improvement, organizational learning, automation risk, control
  effectiveness, evidence-based change, and post-implementation review.
- Architecture synthesis found one missing Stage 5 integration contract and
  selected the current Release Pack standard as the minimum-complexity owner.
- Twelve representative scenarios distinguish claims from evidence, preserve
  human authority, expose uncertainty and tradeoffs, and support acceptance,
  change, deferral, narrowing, rejection, and retirement without scoring.
- Independent review requested one bounded claim-trace repair in Round 1.
  Round 2 verified that repair and recorded `approved` with no remaining
  finding.
- Final Editor finalized the approved package without semantic or authority
  change.

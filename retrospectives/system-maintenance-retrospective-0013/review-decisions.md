# Review Decisions

## Review Scope

`editorial_knowledge/40_editorial_review_system.md` now includes a compact
review pass for `visual_article_sketchnote`.

The review checks semantic usefulness and genre fit, not artistic quality.

## Sketchnote Review Checks

The sketchnote pass checks:

- whether the sketchnote preserves the article's main meaning;
- whether 4-7 key theses are included;
- whether the key theses are actually present in the article;
- whether author conclusions are preserved without invention;
- whether relationships between points are understandable;
- whether the sheet avoids overload;
- whether handwritten phrases are short, meaningful, and controlled;
- whether the result remains a sketchnote rather than an infographic, poster,
  comic, presentation slide, collage, or decorative picture.

## Prompt Drift Check

When both `sketchnote_brief.md` and `image_prompt.md` exist, review may check:

- continuity from brief to prompt;
- preservation of key points and author conclusions;
- preservation of controlled handwritten content;
- one-sheet handwritten note format;
- drift into ordinary illustration, corporate infographic, poster, comic,
  photorealistic scene, collage, or AI-looking decoration.

## Boundaries

The review explicitly avoids:

- OCR requirements;
- artistic quality scoring;
- drawing critique;
- composition review;
- design-system work;
- complex sketchnote methodology.

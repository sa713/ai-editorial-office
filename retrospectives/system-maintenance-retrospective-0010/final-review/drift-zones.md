# Drift Zones

## Compact Abuse

Agents may select compact because it is faster, not because it is safe.

Signal: compact used with factual claims, unresolved questions, or weak brief.

## Optional Artifact Stigma

After normalization, agents may avoid optional artifacts even when useful.

Signal: blockers, evidence gaps, or human approval concerns are buried in
`review.md` or manifest.

## Manifest Over-Reliance

The manifest now carries restart, current state, execution profile, version
pointer, and governance visibility.

Signal: manifest becomes stale or tries to replace status/history.

## Shallow Review

`review.md` as the sole review artifact may become a short verdict without
enough evidence.

Signal: approved reviews with no checked artifacts, no independence basis, or no
reader-outcome validation.

## Skipped Evidence Reading

Short context path may be mistaken for "do not read evidence".

Signal: source-sensitive tasks reviewed from draft only.

## Restart Guessing

Version discipline blocks this, but refinement loops can still tempt agents to
pick newest files.

Signal: "latest" or modified time used instead of manifest pointer.

## Hidden Context Loss

Fewer handoffs can hide useful transfer detail.

Signal: next role cannot explain why the current artifact is safe to use.

## Role Boundary Compression

Shorter role specs may make weak agents rely on general knowledge.

Signal: Reviewer rewrites; Final Editor approves governance; Research drafts.

## Diagnostic Flattening

Compact mode may under-read diagnostic materials.

Signal: author-facing diagnosis becomes generic advice or solution design.

# Governance Safety Review

## Review Discipline

Preserved.

`review.md` remains mandatory. Compact execution explicitly says it does not
skip review, weaken review, or reduce quality.

Residual risk: compact review can become shallow if reviewers treat short form
as a weaker standard. The guard is present, but production behavior must prove
it.

## Governance

Preserved.

Chief Editor final governance remains required. Compact execution does not imply
publication, delivery, release, or human approval.

High-governance work still requires expanded/full depth.

## Role Separation

Preserved.

Compressed role specs still keep the key boundaries:

- Chief Editor coordinates and governs;
- Writer drafts;
- UX Writer owns interface copy;
- Research provides evidence;
- Review validates independently;
- Final Editor finalizes after review.

Residual risk: shorter role specs rely more on canonical references. A role that
does not read `AGENTS.md` and the selected pipeline may miss context.

## Traceability

Preserved, but more conditional.

Source/evidence artifacts remain required when factual, source-sensitive,
high-governance, or reviewer/governance traceability needs exist.

Risk: agents may under-create evidence artifacts if they over-focus on compact
mode.

## Escalation Safety

Improved in version-heavy restarts.

Restart must stop when current version is unclear. It must not guess from latest
modified time.

## Net Assessment

No governance weakening found. The main safety dependency is disciplined
expansion from compact to expanded when uncertainty appears.

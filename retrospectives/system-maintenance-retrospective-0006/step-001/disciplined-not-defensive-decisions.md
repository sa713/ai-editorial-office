# Disciplined, not defensive decisions

## Placement

The principle was added to `editorial_knowledge/01_principles.md` immediately after `Rawness as analytical material`, because it qualifies how diagnostic caution should behave without changing modes, review rules, or execution pipelines.

## Editorial decision

Diagnostic caution is framed as proportionality: the strength of the conclusion should match the available materials.

This means:

- uncertainty is retained where it changes the conclusion, risk, recommendation, or next step;
- strong conclusions remain acceptable when the materials support them;
- repeated disclaimers should not weaken otherwise useful analysis;
- caution must not turn `diagnostic_analysis` into defensive mode.

## Guardrail decision

The principle explicitly preserves the Artificial Concept Completion guardrail. It does not permit unsupported governance, lifecycle, roadmap, process structure, or operating logic.

## Boundary decision

No scoring, confidence matrix, evidence taxonomy, review workflow, or formal escalation structure was introduced.

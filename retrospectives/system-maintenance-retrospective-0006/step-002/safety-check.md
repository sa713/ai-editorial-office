- Observable defensive failure pattern added: yes.

## Diagnostic discipline

The pattern preserves diagnostic discipline by asking the editor to match conclusion strength to material strength.

## Uncertainty boundary

Uncertainty remains allowed. The pattern only rejects repeated, unlocalized, or usefulness-reducing caveats.

## Usefulness protection

The repair moves restore operational conclusions, consolidate uncertainty, and keep strong supported conclusions strong.

## Anti-completion boundary

The `Do not over-correct` section explicitly keeps the guardrail against invented governance, roadmap, lifecycle, process structure, or completion behavior.

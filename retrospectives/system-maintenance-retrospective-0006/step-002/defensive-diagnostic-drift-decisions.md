# Defensive Diagnostic Drift decisions

## Placement

The pattern was added immediately after `Artificial Concept Completion` because it describes the opposite failure: overcorrecting against completion until diagnostic analysis becomes defensive and less useful.

## Pattern shape

The block follows the existing failure-pattern structure:

- observable symptoms;
- usefulness risk;
- likely sources;
- concrete repair moves;
- detection questions;
- explicit overcorrection boundary.

## Editorial decision

The pattern protects diagnostic discipline, not speculative confidence. It says supported conclusions should stay strong, while weak or unsupported inferences should still be marked.

## Guardrail decision

Artificial Concept Completion safeguards remain intact. The new pattern does not permit invented governance, roadmap, lifecycle, process structure, or completion behavior.

## Boundary decision

No new review workflow, confidence framework, scoring system, matrix, or formal evidence logic was introduced.

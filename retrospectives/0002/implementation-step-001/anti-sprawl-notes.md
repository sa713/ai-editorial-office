# Anti-sprawl notes

## Behavioral bureaucracy avoided

No new required fields, artifacts, stages, reviews, or task states were added.

The implementation adds one boundary section only.

## Tone policing avoided

The new text does not ask reviewers or writers to make language warmer, softer, friendlier, or more emotional.

It ties reader-state only to first step, pressure, obligation, evidence, and operational language.

## Emotional editing avoided

No emotional categories, emotional scoring, personality assumptions, or motivational language were added.

## Orchestration growth avoided

No intake prompts or orchestration fields were added.

Reader-state remains a boundary, not a planning form.

## Persuasion drift avoided

The new section explicitly forbids persuasion, engagement optimization, adoption optimization, and implied social proof without evidence.

## Review growth avoided

No review block, pressure audit, review checklist, or reviewer procedure was added. Those belong, if at all, to later steps.

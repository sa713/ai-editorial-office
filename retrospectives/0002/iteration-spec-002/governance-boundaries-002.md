# Governance boundaries

## Core rule

```text
Mandatory stays mandatory.
Optional stays optional.
Unknown stays unknown.
```

Reader-state awareness cannot change the truth of the task.

## Mandatory actions

If an action is required, the text may make it clearer and less intimidating, but must not imply that it is optional.

## Optional actions

If an action is optional, the text must not create fake obligation, social pressure or guilt.

## Unknown facts

If usage, activity, popularity, results or timing are unknown, do not imply them.

## Reader-state edits

Reader-state edits cannot:

- hide rules;
- hide constraints;
- hide approval requirements;
- soften blockers into preferences;
- remove necessary warnings;
- change publication or delivery approval.

## Low-pressure entry

Low-pressure entry is allowed only when factually true.

Allowed:

- "можно сначала посмотреть";
- "можно открыть раздел";
- "можно вернуться позже".

Not allowed:

- presenting a required confirmation as optional;
- suggesting no action is needed when action is required.

## Workspace framing

Workspace framing cannot invent activity.

Allowed:

- describe what exists and where to enter;
- describe what the reader can see;
- describe possible use cases.

Not allowed:

- "коллеги уже активно пользуются" without evidence;
- "пространство живёт" without evidence;
- fake momentum.

## Social proof

Social proof requires evidence and human approval to use.

No evidence means no social proof.

## Manipulation boundary

No psychological manipulation.

No engagement optimization.

No adoption pressure disguised as care.

No guilt, urgency or popularity claims without operational reason and evidence.

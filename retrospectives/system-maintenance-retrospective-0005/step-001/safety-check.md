## Behavioral boundary

The added mode:

- diagnoses raw idea materials;
- marks gaps and uncertainty;
- separates given, implied, and inferred content;
- gives recommendations without designing the final solution;
- states that unsupported claims cannot be asserted as facts.

## Risk check

The main risk is that future users treat recommendations as a design brief. The mode reduces that risk by requiring hypotheses, clarification paths, and explicit boundaries.

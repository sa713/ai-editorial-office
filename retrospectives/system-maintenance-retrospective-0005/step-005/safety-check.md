# Safety check

## Requested constraints

- Rawness principle added: yes.
- New mode created: no.
- Pipelines changed: no.
- Templates changed: no.
- New workflow created: no.
- Maturity model introduced: no.
- Scoring added: no.
- Innovation framework introduced: no.
- Existing principles rewritten: no.
- Philosophy expanded excessively: no.

## Behavioral check

The principle states that:

- incomplete, ambiguous, weakly structured, and early-stage ideas can be valid analytical material;
- uncertainty, gaps, and unfinished structure can be valid analytical outcomes;
- editorial work should understand, distinguish, and diagnose before suggesting development;
- artificial governance, lifecycle, roadmap, process structure, or operating logic should not be added without support.

## Consulting-system check

The addition does not ask the editor to design a finished concept, maturity path, or operating model. It allows recommendations only as questions, directions, hypotheses, or material-level improvements.

 ## Usefulness over completeness
 
+## Rawness as analytical material
+
+Incomplete, ambiguous, weakly structured, or early-stage ideas can be valid objects of editorial analysis.
+
+The editorial task is not automatically to turn them into mature concepts. First understand, distinguish, and diagnose what is present, what is unclear, and what would need confirmation. Only then suggest development.
+
+Uncertainty, gaps, and unfinished structure may be a valid analytical outcome, not a defect to fix.
+
+Do not add governance, lifecycle, roadmap, process structure, or operating logic when the materials do not support them.
+
+Recommendations are allowed as questions, directions, hypotheses, or material-level improvements. They must not replace the author's raw idea with a finished concept.
+
+Interpretation, hypotheses, and suggestions are not forbidden. The failure is hidden completion: making the idea look more mature than the materials justify.

# Rawness tolerance decisions

## Placement

The principle was added to `editorial_knowledge/01_principles.md`, after `Usefulness over completeness`.

No second file was needed.

## Core decision

Rawness is treated as analytical material, not as an automatic editorial defect. Incomplete, ambiguous, weakly structured, and early-stage ideas can be valid subjects for diagnosis.

## Editorial sequence

The principle establishes analysis-before-completion:

1. Understand what is present.
2. Distinguish what is unclear.
3. Diagnose what needs confirmation.
4. Only then suggest development.

## Boundaries

The principle blocks artificial maturity: governance, lifecycle, roadmap, process structure, and operating logic should not be added when unsupported by materials.

Recommendations remain allowed as questions, directions, hypotheses, or material-level improvements, but not as finished concept replacement.

## Anti-overcorrection

Interpretation, hypotheses, and suggestions are not banned. The target failure is hidden completion: making a raw idea appear more mature than the materials justify.

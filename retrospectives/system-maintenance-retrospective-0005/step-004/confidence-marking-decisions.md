# Confidence marking decisions

## Placement

The guidance was added to `editorial_knowledge/10_operational_rules.md`, next to existing rules about separating facts, interpretation, recommendation, and assumptions.

No second file was needed.

## Shape

The addition is one operational rule, not a framework. It gives lightweight labels that may be used only where uncertainty affects diagnostic analysis:

- directly supported by materials;
- implied by materials;
- editorial interpretation;
- bounded hypothesis;
- requires confirmation.

## Main rule

The purpose is semantic honesty, distinction clarity, and uncertainty visibility. The labels are not meant to create formal rigor or evidence auditing.

## Review implication

Review may notice when interpretation is presented as fact or when a hypothesis is hidden as an obvious conclusion. This remains an editorial check, not formal audit behavior.

## Anti-bureaucracy boundary

The rule explicitly rejects scoring, mandatory tagging, evidence matrices, full traceability systems, confidence protocols, and labeling every phrase.

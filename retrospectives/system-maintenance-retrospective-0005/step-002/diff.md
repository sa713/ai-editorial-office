 #### Do not over-correct
 
 Do not make every change sound dramatic. Be direct and proportional.
+
+### Pattern: Artificial Concept Completion
+
+#### What it is
+
+The editorial system completes a raw idea into a more mature concept than the materials support.
+
+#### How it appears
+
+Governance, process, lifecycle, roadmap, pilot, metrics, roles, or solution structure appear without source support. Gaps are smoothed over. Interpretation is presented as part of the author's idea. The material starts to look too complete, too coherent, or too implementation-ready.
+
+#### Why it hurts usefulness
+
+It replaces diagnosis with design. The reader loses the real state of the materials and may mistake synthetic structure for the author's actual idea.
+
+#### Typical source
+
+Completion instinct, discomfort with ambiguity, usefulness overreach, or pressure to produce polished output.
+
+#### Repair move
+
+Separate observed, implied, and inferred material. Restore uncertainty where the source is unclear. Mark hypotheses as hypotheses. Remove invented structure. Ask clarification questions instead of completing the concept.
+
+Detection questions: is this present in the materials? Is the editorial system analyzing or designing? Did this structure come from the author or the model? Can the claim be proven from the source material?
+
+#### Do not over-correct
+
+Do not ban all interpretation or recommendations. Keep bounded hypotheses and material-level recommendations when they are clearly marked and do not become a finished project.

# Artificial Concept Completion decisions

## Placement

The pattern was added to `editorial_knowledge/50_editorial_failure_patterns.md`, the requested failure-pattern registry.

No other file needed edits.

## Shape

The pattern follows the existing structure:

- what it is;
- how it appears;
- why it hurts usefulness;
- typical source;
- repair move;
- do not over-correct.

Detection questions were included as operational review prompts inside the repair area rather than as a new review block.

## Diagnostic focus

The pattern targets observable signals:

- unsupported governance, process, lifecycle, roadmap, pilot, metrics, roles, or solution structure;
- gaps smoothed into coherence;
- model interpretation presented as the author's idea;
- raw material becoming too mature or implementation-ready.

## Repair stance

The repair keeps uncertainty as an acceptable result. The editor should separate observed, implied, and inferred material; mark hypotheses; remove invented structure; and ask clarification questions instead of completing the concept.

## Boundary

The pattern remains editorial. It does not introduce a formal evidence framework, research methodology, scoring model, new workflow, or confidence-marking system.

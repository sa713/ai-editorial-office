# Safety check

## Requested constraints

- New failure pattern added: yes.
- Pipelines changed: no.
- Templates changed: no.
- Review blocks added: no.
- Confidence marking added: no.
- New workflow created: no.
- Existing failure patterns rewritten: no.
- Research methodology introduced: no.
- Steps 3-5 started: no.

## Behavioral check

The pattern helps review detect when the model:

- completes a raw idea;
- raises apparent maturity;
- removes uncertainty;
- adds unsupported solution, governance, lifecycle, or process structure;
- replaces analysis with design.

## Operational check

The pattern gives observable signals and repair moves, not vague style guidance. It keeps the smallest repair path: separate source-supported material from implication and inference, restore uncertainty, remove invented structure, and ask clarification questions.

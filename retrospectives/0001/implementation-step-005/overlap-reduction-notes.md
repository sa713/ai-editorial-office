# Overlap reduction notes

## Reduced overlap

- Compact review can keep checklist and summary content inside `review.md`.
- `qa-checklist.md` is separate only when review depth or risk needs detailed checks.
- `review-summary.md` is separate only when concise transfer is not already clear in `review.md` and handoff.
- `reviewer-notes.md` is separate only when extra caveats or borderline reasoning do not fit in `review.md`.
- Handoff remains transfer delta, not full review summary.

## Preserved boundaries

- `review.md` remains the required review artifact.
- `qa-checklist.md` remains available for normal/full review.
- `review-summary.md` remains available when a downstream role needs concise routing.
- Review findings stay out of manifest and status except for compact state/routing.

## Remaining risk

Some task templates still show larger review artifact sets. This step did not rewrite task templates to avoid broad template churn.

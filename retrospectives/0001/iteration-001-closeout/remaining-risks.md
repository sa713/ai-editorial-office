# Remaining risks

## Architectural weak points

- Review independence is still textual, not mechanically proven.
- Manifest freshness depends on discipline.
- Compact path depends on correct risk judgment.
- Custom workflow promotion remains manual.
- Legacy tasks still contain older naming and artifact patterns.

## Places likely to drift again

- `project-state.md`, because it can accumulate temporary decisions.
- Task templates, because they can turn conditional artifacts into default lists.
- Pipelines, because task-type notes may restate AGENTS rules.
- Review artifacts, because checklist and summary overlap easily.
- Handoffs, because role transfer can expand into restart summary.

## Where bureaucracy could regrow

- Compact review could recreate full checklist in every case.
- Governance visibility fields could become approval matrix.
- Custom workflow mini-contract could become a default section for ordinary tasks.
- Source trust could become excessive labeling.
- Production stabilization notes could become roadmap.

## Where semantics may still blur

- `compact` as process depth vs compact handoff.
- Finalization vs publication/delivery approval.
- Handoff vs context-summary.
- Review summary vs review handoff.
- Bounded revision vs full rewrite.

## Current posture

These risks do not require new systems now. They require observation in real production tasks and correction by trimming, not expanding.

# Author concept heuristics decisions

The heuristics were added as a bounded review aid for `author_concept_diagnosis`, not as a framework or methodology.

Key decisions:

- Place the block after the existing `diagnostic_analysis` bounded pass, because it is also mode-specific review guidance.
- Keep each heuristic as a question-like conceptual distinction.
- Focus on helping the author see what is undefined, mixed, absent, disconnected, or not distinguished.
- Include the requested distinctions: central thought clarity, problem vs solution confusion, mechanism visibility, audience definition, expected change, success criterion, boundary clarity, and conceptual mixing.
- Explicitly prohibit mandatory checklist behavior, consulting canvas drift, maturity model use, taxonomy-system expansion, scoring, and artificial sophistication.

Boundary decisions:

- The heuristics support author-facing diagnosis only.
- They do not ask the editorial system to invent the author's concept.
- They do not turn the editorial system into an ideation consultant.

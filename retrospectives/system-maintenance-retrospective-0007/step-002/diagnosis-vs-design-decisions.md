# Diagnosis vs design decisions

The distinction was added as one operational rule, not as a new workflow or methodology.

Key decisions:

- Editorial diagnosis is framed around the material as an object of editorial work.
- Author concept diagnosis is framed around the author's idea state.
- Solution design is framed as legitimate work, but only when explicitly requested or recorded in the task.
- The rule uses working questions to keep the distinction easy to apply.
- Repair guidance stays lightweight: return to the requested work type, name analysis versus interpretation versus proposed development, and remove unrequested ready-made solutions.

Boundary decisions:

- Diagnosis may identify gaps, uncertainty, and possible clarification directions.
- Diagnosis must not silently become design.
- Design is not prohibited, but it must be named as a separate next step when it was not part of the original task.

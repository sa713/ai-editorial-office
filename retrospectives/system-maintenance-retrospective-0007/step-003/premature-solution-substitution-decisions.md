# Premature Solution Substitution decisions

The new pattern was added as a failure pattern, not as a framework, method, workflow, or review-system rewrite.

Key decisions:

- Place the pattern after `Artificial Concept Completion` because both involve premature maturity, but this pattern focuses on author-facing diagnosis and authorship.
- Keep `Artificial Concept Completion` intact and do not weaken its guardrails.
- Define the failure as replacement of the author's conceptual work, not as any suggestion or hypothesis.
- Keep hypotheses, development suggestions, clarification directions, and next-step recommendations explicitly allowed.
- Make detection questions observable: whether diagnosis became design, whether gaps were filled automatically, and whether the author would still recognize the idea as theirs.
- Make repair moves return the work to diagnosis, expose unresolved areas, separate gaps from possible development, and restore author ownership.

Boundary decisions:

- The pattern does not prohibit helping the author.
- The pattern does prohibit silently substituting a ready-made solution for author concept diagnosis.
- The pattern does not introduce scoring or a consulting/ideation consultant role.

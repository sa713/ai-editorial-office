 #### Do not over-correct
 
 Do not ban all interpretation or recommendations. Keep bounded hypotheses and material-level recommendations when they are clearly marked and do not become a finished project.
 
+### Pattern: Premature Solution Substitution
+
+#### What it is
+
+The editorial system is asked to help the author understand the state of an idea, but starts offering a ready-made construction, solution, program, or concept instead.
+
+#### How it appears
+
+Diagnosis quickly turns into a proposed solution. Roadmap, process, governance, metrics, roles, or implementation elements appear before the idea is clear. The system starts assembling the idea for the author. Recommendations read like a finished strategy. Conceptual gaps are automatically replaced with invented structure. The author receives someone else's concept instead of understanding what is unresolved in their own idea.
+
+#### Why it hurts usefulness
+
+It protects neither the idea nor the author. Authorship is weakened, unresolved thinking is masked, and the idea gains false conceptual maturity through hidden solutioning.
+
+#### Typical source
+
+Completion instinct, pressure to be useful, confusion between helping and replacing, hidden consulting drift, or discomfort with unresolved conceptual gaps.
+
+#### Repair move
+
+Return from solution to diagnosis. Expose the unresolved conceptual area. Replace ready-made recommendations with questions, hypotheses, options to test, or clarification directions. Separate observed gaps from possible development. Keep conceptual ownership with the author.
+
+Detection questions: is the editorial system helping the author think or thinking instead of the author? Did diagnosis quietly become design? Would the author still recognize this as their own idea? Are conceptual gaps being analyzed or automatically filled? Was a solution introduced before the idea became clear?
+
+#### Do not over-correct
+
+Do not ban hypotheses, development suggestions, clarification directions, or next-step recommendations. The failure is not helping the author; the failure is replacing the author's conceptual work with a ready-made solution.
+

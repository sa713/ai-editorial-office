# Author concept diagnosis decisions

The new mode was added as an editorial interaction mode, not as a workflow or agent behavior.

Key decisions:

- Center the reader as the author of the idea.
- Diagnose the state of the idea itself, not the quality of the written material.
- Distinguish this mode from `diagnostic_analysis`, which focuses on raw materials, implications, uncertainty, and material-level recommendations.
- Require separation of problem, intention, mechanism, audience, expected change, and success criterion.
- Make undefined, mixed, and boundary-less parts visible without resolving them for the author.
- Preserve author ownership by framing development as questions, testable options, clarification directions, or hypotheses.

Explicit non-decisions:

- No concept completion.
- No solution design.
- No strategy writing.
- No governance, roadmap, lifecycle, pilot, metrics, or finished program.
- No consulting or coaching posture.

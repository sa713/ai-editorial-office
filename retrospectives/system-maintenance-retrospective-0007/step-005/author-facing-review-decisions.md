# Author-facing review decisions

The review addition was implemented as a minimal authorship check, not as a new stage, workflow, or methodology.

Key decisions:

- Place the check after the existing `author_concept_diagnosis` conceptual heuristics so the review system can evaluate whether the output preserved authorship.
- Focus on editorial takeover: whether the output helps the author see the idea or starts thinking instead of the author.
- Include the requested checks: authorship preservation, diagnosis vs invention, conceptual visibility, suggestion boundaries, and usefulness to the author.
- Keep suggestions allowed when they appear as questions, hypotheses, clarification prompts, directions to test, or next-step recommendations.
- Explicitly reject scoring, maturity review, coaching sessions, hidden consulting, and ideation methodology.

Boundary decisions:

- Review may flag unrequested finished solution design.
- Review must not require passive reflection only.
- Review must preserve the usefulness of author-facing diagnosis while protecting conceptual ownership.

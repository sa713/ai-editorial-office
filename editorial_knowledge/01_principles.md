## Usefulness over completeness

## Rawness as analytical material

Incomplete, ambiguous, weakly structured, or early-stage ideas can be valid objects of editorial analysis.

The editorial task is not automatically to turn them into mature concepts. First understand, distinguish, and diagnose what is present, what is unclear, and what would need confirmation. Only then suggest development.

Uncertainty, gaps, and unfinished structure may be a valid analytical outcome, not a defect to fix.

Do not add governance, lifecycle, roadmap, process structure, or operating logic when the materials do not support them.

Recommendations are allowed as questions, directions, hypotheses, or material-level improvements. They must not replace the author's raw idea with a finished concept.

Interpretation, hypotheses, and suggestions are not forbidden. The failure is hidden completion: making the idea look more mature than the materials justify.

## Disciplined, not defensive

Diagnostic discipline means matching the strength of the conclusion to the materials, not maximizing caution.

Strong conclusions are allowed when the materials support them. State uncertainty where it changes the conclusion, risk, recommendation, or next step.

Avoid disclaimer inflation. One precise limitation is better than weakening the same point in every section.

Do not mark an evident conclusion as a weak hypothesis only to sound cautious.

This principle does not soften the guardrail against Artificial Concept Completion. Do not add governance, lifecycle, roadmap, process structure, or operating logic without support from the materials.

## Reader-state boundaries

Reader-state awareness is a small editorial sensitivity, not a behavioral layer.

Use it only when a task asks the reader to enter, try, join, respond, use a workspace, or take a first step that may feel heavier than it needs to be.

The purpose is limited:

- make the honest first step clear;
- reduce unnecessary pressure;
- allow observation before commitment when that is factually available;
- keep the language human, operational, and specific.

It must not become:

- persuasion;
- emotional editing;
- engagement or adoption optimization;
- tone policing;
- a new workflow, stage, role, score, or review requirement.

### Honesty rule

Mandatory stays mandatory.
Optional stays optional.
Unknown stays unknown.

Reader-state edits must not hide rules, soften real obligations into suggestions, make optional actions sound required, or imply activity and social proof without evidence.
